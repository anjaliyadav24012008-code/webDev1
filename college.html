MY COLLEGE:MIRAI SCHOOL OF TECHNOLOGY

Mirai School of Technology is an AI-first undergraduate engineering institute founded in 2025 that offers practical, portfolio-driven
B.Tech programs in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML).

  • Overview & Campuses
  • curriculum & Infrastructure

Overview & Campuses

  • Founders: Created by Arpit Sarda (ex-JP Morgan and BITS Pilani alumnus), alongside Varun Kohli and Kartik Mathur (the founders of Coding Blocks).

  • Partner Campuses: Delivers its programs in collaboration with AICTE- and UGC-approved universities, including the Hi-Tech Institute of Engineering & Technology in Ghaziabad (Delhi-NCR), Rathinam Institute of Technology in Bengaluru, and Jagannath University in Jaipur.

  • Admissions: Selective process targeting top-tier merit applicants through an online application, a MAINS logic test, and a personal interview.

Curriculum & Infrastructure

  • Day-One Coding: Focuses on practical engineering stacks, Large Language Models (LLMs), and full-stack development starting in the first semester rather than waiting until later years.

  • Hardware & Labs: Provides students with a pre-configured, high-performance MacBook from Day 1 alongside access to specialized development environments like Apple Labs and robotics facilities.

  • Mentorship: Features active tech practitioners and engineers from global firms like Google, Amazon, Adobe, and Microsoft as direct mentors and instructors.
